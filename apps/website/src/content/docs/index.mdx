Quipthread adds threaded comments, sign-in, and moderation to your website.

## Choose how to run it

| Option | What you manage | Start here |
|---|---|---|
| Quipthread Cloud | Your sites and comments | [Set up a cloud account](/docs/cloud/getting-started/) |
| Self-host with Docker | Your server, domain, and backups | [Run with Docker](/docs/guides/docker/) |

Docker includes the app, dashboard, and migration tools. If you need to build from source, follow the [source-build guide](/docs/guides/self-hosting/).

A self-hosted instance serves one site and includes moderation, blocked terms, Turnstile bot protection, import, JSON and CSV export, and SMTP email notifications. Analytics, multiple sites, and team members are available on Quipthread Cloud.

## Add comments to your website

Create a site in the dashboard and copy its embed snippet into your page. The [embed guide](/docs/embed/reference/) explains the required attributes; [themes](/docs/embed/theming/) control its appearance.

## Manage your comments

- [Moderation](/docs/dashboard/moderation/): approve, reject, edit, reply, and review flagged comments.
- [Import and export](/docs/dashboard/import-export/): move comments between platforms.
- [Security and spam](/docs/dashboard/security/): manage abuse and bot protection.
- [Notifications](/docs/guides/notifications/): receive moderation digests by email.

If you self-host, set up [backups and restore](/docs/guides/backups/) before relying on the instance.
