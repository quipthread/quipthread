Quipthread emails you when new comments need moderation. Comments are grouped into digests to avoid a message for every comment, and each comment in a digest has links to approve or reject it straight from the email.

Comments that are approved automatically, from commenters you have approved before, do not trigger a notification.

## Quipthread Cloud

Digests go to the email address on the account that owns the site. There is nothing to configure.

Comments are collected in 5-minute windows, and each window's digest goes out when the window closes, so you hear about a new comment within about 5 minutes. On the Pro plan and above, you can change the window per site on the **Sites** page under **Notifications**: 1, 5, 15, or 30 minutes, or 1 hour.

## Self-hosted

Self-hosted instances send digests over SMTP, using the same settings as [email sign-in](/docs/guides/email-auth/):

```dotenv
SMTP_HOST=smtp.example.com
SMTP_USER=user@example.com
SMTP_PASS=your-password
SMTP_FROM=noreply@example.com

# Optional: recipient when the site owner's account has no email address
NOTIFY_EMAIL_TO=you@example.com
```

A digest goes out as soon as comments are waiting if no digest was sent in the last 5 minutes. Otherwise it waits until 5 minutes have passed, or until `NOTIFY_BATCH_SIZE` comments are waiting, whichever comes first.

```dotenv
NOTIFY_BATCH_SIZE=5      # default: 5
```

<Aside>
  Notifications need working SMTP settings. Setting `NOTIFY_EMAIL_TO` alone does not send anything.
</Aside>
