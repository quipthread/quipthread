Quipthread Cloud is the managed version of Quipthread. You get a fully hosted backend — no server to run, no binary to build.

## 1. Sign up

Go to [app.quipthread.com/signup](https://app.quipthread.com/signup). The Hobby plan is free and requires no credit card.

## 2. Create your first site

After you sign in, the onboarding wizard asks for your website's domain, such as `myblog.com`. Enter it and select **Continue**. The next step shows your **Site ID** and an embed snippet for your framework.

You can select **Skip for now** and add a site later from the **Sites** page.

## 3. Add the embed snippet

Copy the snippet from the onboarding wizard, or later from the **Preview** page in the dashboard, and paste it into any page where you want comments. Place the `<div>` where the widget should appear, then add the `<script>` tag:

```html
<div
  data-quipthread
  data-site-id="YOUR_SITE_ID"
  data-page-id="YOUR_PAGE_ID"
></div>
<script
  src="https://app.quipthread.com/embed.js"
  async
></script>
```

Replace `YOUR_SITE_ID` with the ID from the dashboard, and set `data-page-id` to a unique string per page (e.g. the post slug or URL path). Use the script address supplied by the dashboard.

## 4. Moderate comments

A commenter's first comment on your site waits in the moderation queue. Open the **Comments** page in the dashboard to approve, reject, or reply. After you approve someone once, their later comments on that site go live immediately.

<Aside>
  To upgrade your plan, open the **Billing** page in the dashboard.
</Aside>

## Plan limits

| Plan | Comments / month | Sites |
|---|---|---|
| Hobby | 1,000 | 1 |
| Starter | 10,000 | 5 |
| Pro | 50,000 | 20 |
| Business | 250,000 | Unlimited |
| Enterprise | Unlimited | Unlimited |

Paid plans include a 14-day free trial. For Enterprise, email [hello@quipthread.com](mailto:hello@quipthread.com).

Monthly posting and site limits are enforced for each plan. When you reach a limit, upgrade from **Billing** or wait for the monthly posting allowance to reset. Existing comments remain available. New comments, replies, and imports use this allowance; deleting comments does not restore it. Limits reset on the first day of each month at 00:00 UTC. The Billing page shows your current usage and allowance.
